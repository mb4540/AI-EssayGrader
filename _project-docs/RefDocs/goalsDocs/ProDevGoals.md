Professional Development Goals
Teacher: Shana Busby (Grade 6 ELA, Mansfield ISD)School Year: 2025–2026Focus: Designing and piloting an AI-assisted reading & grading workflow that publishes all student-facing materials and feedback through Canvas and Google Docs (no student access to other systems)
1) Purpose & Rationale
Shana will lead a structured classroom workflow pilot to reduce time spent on routine preparation and grading while improving the alignment of lessons and feedback to TEKS for Grade 6 ELA. The work centers on a teacher tool (no student login) that:
	•	Generates TEKS-aligned lesson materials using the specific books taught in class.
	•	Imports STAAR-formatted items (via approved links/PDFs), maps them to TEKS, and prepares them for use.
	•	Publishes assignments, rubrics, and feedback only through Canvas (and/or Google Docs), consistent with school IT rules.
This pilot directly supports district priorities: TEKS alignment, consistent feedback, data-informed instruction, and efficient use of teacher time.
2) Professional Development Goals for Shana
Goal 1 – Curriculum Design Leadership:Develop the ability to quickly produce and refine TEKS-aligned lesson materials (including differentiated options) tied to the actual texts used in class.
Goal 2 – Assessment Literacy:Curate and adapt STAAR-formatted questions and rubrics; ensure items explicitly reference TEKS strands and require text evidence where appropriate.
Goal 3 – Feedback & Grading Efficiency:Adopt an AI-assisted grading workflow (teacher-in-control) that returns consistent, evidence-based comments to students in Canvas/Docs, reducing turnaround time and increasing clarity.
Goal 4 – Data-Informed Adjustments:Use Canvas/Docs artifacts and simple dashboards to monitor student progress by TEKS strand and to select targeted re-teaching or enrichment.
Professional Growth Evidence (artifacts Shana will collect):
	•	Side-by-side examples of original vs. refined lesson plans with TEKS references.
	•	A set of adapted STAAR-formatted items and corresponding rubrics.
	•	Samples of evidence-based feedback returned in Canvas/Docs.
	•	A reflection log noting instructional adjustments made based on student data and outcomes.
3) Student Benefits & Measurable Outcomes
Benefits to Students
	•	Clearer instructions and rubrics aligned to TEKS and class texts.
	•	Faster, more consistent feedback that points to specific passages and skills.
	•	Differentiated materials (e.g., graphic organizers, simplified language versions, audio read-alouds) delivered in Canvas/Docs.
Success Indicators (tracked each grading period)
	•	Feedback timeliness: Median turnaround time for graded responses/posts in Canvas decreases by 30–50% vs. baseline.
	•	Quality of evidence use: In writing tasks that require text evidence, increase the % of responses that correctly cite relevant passages by 10–20 points.
	•	TEKS mastery growth: On teacher common assessments or exit tickets, increase proficiency on targeted strands (e.g., 6.3A plot, 6.3B text evidence, 6.7 main idea) by 10+ points among students receiving targeted supports.
	•	Student clarity ratings: Bi-monthly 3-question check-in (Canvas form) shows improved student ratings for “I know what is expected” and “Feedback helps me improve.”
4) What Shana Will Do (Plain, Non-Technical)
Shana will serve as the Teacher Lead for the Workflow Pilot:
	1.	Set the goals: Define the unit objectives and the TEKS strands to target each six weeks.
	2.	Select class texts and supply teacher-approved materials (book excerpts, STAAR-formatted items/PDFs).
	3.	Review and edit AI-drafted lesson plans, questions, and rubrics to ensure they match her classroom needs.
	4.	Publish to Canvas/Docs: Post assignments, rubrics, and instructions exclusively in district-approved systems.
	5.	Grade with oversight: Use AI-assisted scoring as a first draft; accept/edit/override as needed.
	6.	Return feedback in Canvas/Docs and allow revisions where appropriate.
	7.	Monitor progress: Look at simple TEKS-by-skill summaries and decide what to re-teach or extend.
	8.	Reflect and refine: Keep brief notes on what worked, what didn’t, and what to adjust next cycle.
Note: Shana will collaborate with a full-stack application developer (a community partner) who will implement requested improvements, ensure data stays within approved systems, and adjust the workflow based on Shana’s ongoing classroom feedback. No student data will flow to unapproved platforms.
5) Timeline (Aug–May)
	•	Aug–Sep (Set-Up & Baseline): Identify 2–3 TEKS focus areas; gather baseline metrics (grading turnaround, rubric clarity, student “clarity” ratings). Prepare initial STAAR-formatted items/PDFs and unit plans.
	•	Oct–Dec (Pilot Cycle 1): Implement the workflow in one core unit; publish via Canvas/Docs; collect artifacts and metrics.
	•	Jan–Mar (Pilot Cycle 2): Expand to an additional unit with refined rubrics and differentiated materials; compare results to baseline and Cycle 1.
	•	Apr–May (Sustain & Share): Document repeatable steps, sample artifacts, and results; prepare a short share-out for the ELA team/administration.
6) Evidence & Reporting to Administration
Shana will submit a short, end-of-semester packet (digital folder) containing:
	•	3–5 representative Canvas/Docs artifacts (instructions, rubric, graded samples with evidence-based comments).
	•	A one-page data summary with baseline vs. end-of-cycle metrics (turnaround time, TEKS mastery indicators, student clarity ratings).
	•	A brief reflection on instructional adjustments and plans for next steps.
7) Supports & Safeguards
	•	IT Compliance: All student-facing materials and feedback remain in Canvas/Docs; no student access to external tools.
	•	Teacher-in-Control: AI suggestions are drafts; Shana approves all content and final grades.
	•	Scope & Time: Start with one unit, then expand based on results to minimize disruption.
	•	Optional Collaboration Touchpoints: Quick 15–20 minute check-ins bi-weekly with the developer to prioritize small improvements Shana requests.

Administrator Summary (for quick review)
	•	What this is: A classroom workflow pilot to speed up prep and grading, while strengthening TEKS alignment—all within Canvas/Docs.
	•	What the teacher gains: Practical skill growth in assessment design, rubric clarity, and feedback efficiency.
	•	What students gain: Clear TEKS-aligned tasks, faster and more helpful feedback, and differentiated supports.
	•	What you’ll see: Short artifact samples and simple before/after metrics each semester, with no new student logins required.
