AI-Assisted Reading & Grading Platform (Teacher-Only)
Audience: Grade 6 ELA – Mansfield ISDStudent Delivery: Canvas and Google Docs only (no student access to this platform)
General Overview
This teacher-only tool helps create and manage TEKS-aligned reading lessons and assessments for specific class books. It streamlines planning, brings in STAAR-formatted items, and speeds up grading with clear, evidence-based feedback—all while keeping student-facing work inside Canvas or Google Docs per district IT rules. The teacher stays in control: AI produces drafts; the teacher reviews, edits, and publishes.
Key Features
	•	Book-Based Planning
	•	Load the novels/texts you actually teach.
	•	Generate lesson ideas and activities aligned to TEKS for Grade 6 ELA.
	•	Produce differentiated versions (simplified text, organizers, audio notes) to support varied learners.
	•	STAAR-Style Assessment Support
	•	Bring in STAAR-formatted practice items by pasting a link or uploading a PDF.
	•	The system organizes questions by TEKS strand and makes them reusable.
	•	Teacher-Controlled Publishing
	•	One-click publish of instructions, rubrics, and materials to Canvas (Assignments/Pages/Modules) or export to Google Docs.
	•	No new student logins; students only see work in Canvas or Docs.
	•	Efficient, Consistent Grading
	•	AI suggests grades and comments tied to rubrics and text evidence.
	•	Teacher reviews/edits/approves before anything is returned.
	•	Send feedback back to Canvas comments or Google Docs suggestions.
	•	Actionable Insights for Re-Teaching
	•	Simple views of class progress by TEKS skill (e.g., plot, text evidence, main idea).
	•	Quick suggestions for who may need a scaffold or extension activity.
	•	Teacher decides what to republish to Canvas/Docs.
	•	Rubrics & Materials Library
	•	Save and reuse rubrics, question sets, organizers, and exemplars.
	•	Tag items by book/chapter and TEKS strand for easy retrieval.
	•	Accessibility & Inclusion
	•	Materials produced with readability and accessibility in mind (e.g., alt text, organized headings).
	•	Supports multiple formats (text, organizer, audio notes) for different learning needs.
	•	Privacy & Compliance
	•	Teacher credentials used for Canvas/Google only.
	•	No student accounts on this tool; student work stays in Canvas/Docs.
	•	Teacher remains the final decision-maker on content and grades.
What’s Not Included
	•	No student-facing website or app. All student work and communication happen through Canvas or Google Docs only.
